## Project Overview

This project was developed as part of DSC 640 coursework at Bellevue University. It uses Microsoft Power BI to analyze Netflix's global content performance, audience engagement, and viewing trends across countries and show titles.

The interactive dashboard, **Netflix Global Viewing Insights**, presents key performance indicators and visual comparisons to explore which shows attract the most viewing activity, achieve broad audience reach, and maintain popularity over time.

## Business and Analytical Objectives

- Analyze Netflix viewing performance across countries and show titles.
- Identify top-performing shows based on viewing hours and audience reach.
- Examine how long popular titles remain in the Top 10 rankings.
- Compare viewing hours for English-language and non-English-language content.
- Enable interactive exploration by country, category, show title, and reporting period.
- Communicate global content performance through interactive business intelligence visualizations.

## Data Sources

The project uses three Excel datasets containing Netflix viewing and popularity information:

- `all-weeks-countries-netflix.xlsx` — country-level weekly Top 10 data.
- `all-weeks-global-netflix.xlsx` — global weekly viewing and ranking data.
- `most-popular-netflix.xlsx` — information about Netflix's most popular titles.

These datasets support the analysis of global viewing patterns, title performance, and audience engagement.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Power BI Data Modeling** — organizing and relating data for analysis.

## Dashboard

### Netflix Global Viewing Insights

The dashboard includes:

**Key Performance Indicators**
- 94 unique countries.
- 1,915 unique show titles.
- Average watch time per view: 4.80.

**Audience Engagement**
- Top shows by hours viewed during the previous 91 days.
- Comparison of viewing activity across popular titles.

**Audience Reach**
- Top shows based on reported views during the previous 91 days.
- Identification of content reaching the largest audiences.

**Content Popularity**
- Number of weeks individual titles appeared in Netflix's Top 10 rankings.
- Comparison of sustained popularity across titles.

**Language Categories**
- Distribution of viewing hours between English-language and non-English-language categories.

**Interactive Filtering**
- Country.
- Category.
- Show title.
- Week date.

## Key Analytical Insights

The dashboard demonstrates how content performance can be evaluated using several complementary measures.

- **Viewing hours** measure total content consumption and help identify titles generating substantial engagement.
- **Audience reach** provides a different perspective on popularity by examining reported views.
- **Weeks in the Top 10** help illustrate how consistently titles remain among Netflix's most popular content.
- **Language-category comparisons** provide insight into viewing activity across English-language and non-English-language content.

Together, these measures provide a more complete perspective on content performance than relying on a single popularity metric.

## Limitations

- Results reflect the reporting periods available in the source datasets.
- Viewing hours and reported views are different measures and should not be interpreted interchangeably.
- Historical popularity does not necessarily predict future content performance.
- Differences in title duration, reporting periods, and categories can affect comparisons.
- The dashboard provides descriptive analysis rather than causal conclusions about viewing behavior.
